# Contributing to Jarri Workspace Core

Jarri Workspace Core is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0
(MPL-2.0); see [`LICENSE`](LICENSE). Before proposing a change, review the
license and the current architecture and persistence contracts linked from
the [documentation index](docs/README.md).

## Licensing changes

- The first-party Core source covered by the MPL remains subject to the MPL
  when source modifications to those files are distributed. Keep existing
  license and attribution notices intact.
- Identify copied or adapted third-party code, artwork, palettes, fonts, and
  other assets, and preserve their separate notices and license conditions.
  Do not assume that a file is MPL-covered just because it is in this
  repository.
- Independent modules may carry their own compatible licenses. When they are
  combined or distributed with Core, satisfy the MPL and each other applicable
  license; see MPL section 3.3.
- Do not add trademark or brand assets or imply endorsement without authority
  to do so. MPL-2.0 does not grant trademark, service-mark, or logo rights.
- No contribution agreement or CLA is defined here. Contributors should only
  submit material they have authority to contribute under the applicable
  terms; the project owner should resolve licensing questions before
  accepting uncertain material.

## Downstream use

MPL-2.0 permits commercial use, customization, redistribution, and the sale of
support, customization, and integration services, subject to its terms. A
distributor need not contribute modifications upstream. If distributing
modifications to MPL-covered files in source form, the MPL's conditions apply
to those files. Separate modules can be licensed independently where the
applicable license terms allow it.

## Reporting provenance

When a change includes a third-party contribution, add or update
[`docs/third-party-notices.md`](docs/third-party-notices.md) with its source,
creator attribution, license, and any required notices. If the source or
permission is uncertain, record it as unresolved instead of inferring consent.
